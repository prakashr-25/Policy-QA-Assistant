Company Attendance Policy
Department: HR
Year: 2025

1. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
2. Employees must work 8 hours per day.
3. Employees can take a 1-hour lunch break.
4. Employees must record attendance using the company attendance system.
5. Employees arriving more than 15 minutes late must inform their manager.
6. Repeated late arrivals may result in disciplinary action.
