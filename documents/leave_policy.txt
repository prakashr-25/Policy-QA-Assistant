Company Leave Policy
Department: HR
Year: 2025

1. Employees receive 20 annual leave days per year.
2. Employees receive 12 casual leave days per year.
3. Employees receive 10 sick leave days per year.
4. Casual leave requires approval from the reporting manager.
5. Annual leave requests should be submitted at least 5 working days in advance.
6. Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to 5 days.
7. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal.
